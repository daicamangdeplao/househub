1. Income & Fixed Obligations (Baseline)

This establishes the financial “ground truth” of the household.

Monthly income sources

Salary (net)

Rental income (if applicable)

Other recurring inflows

Fixed recurring costs

Rent / mortgage

Insurance

Internet / phone

Subscriptions

Property taxes (amortized monthly)

➡️ Output: predictable monthly baseline (what must be paid regardless of behavior).

2. Variable Household Expenses (Behavior-Driven)

This is where the system becomes intelligent.

Electricity cost (grid vs solar offset)

Heating / cooling costs

Water (if metered)

Maintenance events

Ad-hoc household spending

Each expense is:

Time-bucketed (daily / monthly)

Categorized

Linked to actual usage data where possible

➡️ Example:
“Electricity cost increased 14% this month, primarily driven by HVAC usage between 18:00–23:00.”

3. Energy-Financial Coupling (Key Differentiator)

This is what separates your system from normal budgeting apps.

Solar production value (kWh × tariff)

Self-consumption vs grid export

Cost savings compared to grid-only usage

ROI tracking of solar installation

➡️ Example:
“This month, solar reduced grid spending by €63.40 and covered 42% of total consumption.”

4. Budgeting & Forecasting

Simple, deterministic, and explainable (not “AI magic”).

Monthly budget targets per category

Forecast end-of-month balance

Scenario simulation:

“What if electricity prices rise 20%?”

“What if we add another EV charger?”

➡️ Output: financial predictability, not just reporting.

5. Automation-Driven Cost Control (IoT Integration)

This is where oversight becomes actionable.

Trigger rules:

If electricity price spikes → reduce non-essential loads

If solar production peaks → schedule heavy devices

Cost-based automation:

Run appliances when marginal cost ≈ 0

Enforce monthly energy spending caps

➡️ Example:
“Dishwasher delayed to 13:20 to use excess solar power, saving €1.80.”

6. Auditability & History (Traditional, Important)

A conservative but essential aspect.

Immutable monthly summaries

Expense history with traceability

Exportable reports (CSV / PDF)

Year-over-year comparison

This makes the system trustworthy, not just smart.
